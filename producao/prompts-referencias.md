## Bloco de estilo (repetir em TODOS os prompts)

```
2D hand-drawn cartoon illustration, stick-figure character style.
White rounded head with thin black outline, simple dot eyes and a small
simple mouth. Torso and limbs are plain thin black lines. Small simple
hands and feet, no realistic fingers. Flat muted colors, soft cream-beige
background, subtle paper texture, soft shadows.
NOT 3D, NOT photorealistic, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT volumetric human
anatomy. Exactly two arms and two legs.
```

## R-04 — Léo com casaco azul-petróleo

**Modelo sugerido:** Nano Banana Pro (consistência de personagem)
**Referência de entrada:** folha de 8 vistas do Léo (fornecida pelo criador)

```
[BLOCO DE ESTILO]

Character sheet, 8 views of the same character in one image, 2 rows of 4,
evenly spaced on a plain cream background.
Character: a stick-figure boy wearing a RED baseball cap with the brim
facing FORWARD, and a flat simple TEAL-BLUE winter coat, zipped closed.
The coat is drawn flat and simple and must NOT make the character look
like a bulky realistic human. A MOSS-GREEN backpack on his back.
Views: front, 3/4 front, side profile, 3/4 back, back, 3/4 back opposite
side, side profile opposite, 3/4 front opposite.
Keep the face, proportions, line weight and drawing style identical to
the attached reference.
```

> ⚠️ Verificar na saída: boné vermelho para a frente, exatamente 2 braços e
> 2 pernas, casaco plano (não volumoso), mochila presente em todas as vistas.

## R-10 — Mochila aberta com o conteúdo

**Modelo sugerido:** GPT Image 2.5 (objetos e legibilidade)

```
[BLOCO DE ESTILO]

Flat lay illustration, top-down view on a forest floor.
An open MOSS-GREEN backpack with its contents laid out neatly beside it:
a folded TEAL-BLUE coat, a folded ORANGE emergency shelter/bivvy, a rolled
sleeping pad, a small silver whistle, a flashlight, a water bottle, and a
wrapped sandwich.
Each object clearly separated and readable. Same hand-drawn cartoon line
style as the characters. Cream background, soft shadows.
```

> ⚠️ O apito precisa estar visível e identificável — é o objeto-chave do Bloco 8.

## R-05 — Urso

**Modelo sugerido:** Nano Banana Pro

```
[BLOCO DE ESTILO — adaptado: o urso NÃO é stick figure]

Character sheet, 3 views of the same bear in one image: side profile,
3/4 front, and front.
An adult DARK BROWN bear, 2D hand-drawn cartoon illustration with thin
black outlines and flat muted colors. Lighter muzzle, rounded ears.
Recognizable real bear anatomy, walking on four legs.
NO clothing, NO human features, NOT anthropomorphic, NOT cute mascot
style, NOT standing on two legs.
Same line weight and paper texture as the character sheets.
Cream background.
```

> ⚠️ Este é o único personagem que **não** é boneco de palitos (roteiro, linha 50).

A small ORANGE emergency shelter (a simple tarp/bivvy) pitched low between

## R-06 — Camping autorizado

**Modelo sugerido:** GPT Image 2.5

```
[BLOCO DE ESTILO]

Wide establishing shot of a small authorized campsite in a temperate
North American forest.
A wooden picnic table, a GREEN tent, a METAL FIRE RING on the ground, and
a BUCKET OF WATER beside the fire ring. Tall pine trees in the background.
Evening light. 2D hand-drawn cartoon illustration, thin black outlines,

```

> ⚠️ O anel metálico e o balde precisam aparecer — sustentam a regra de que o
> fogo só é permitido aqui (Bloco 5).

## R-07 — Clareira A

**Modelo sugerido:** GPT Image 2.5

```
[BLOCO DE ESTILO]

Wide establishing shot of a small forest clearing.
A distinctive pine tree with a CURVED EXPOSED ROOT in the BACK-LEFT of the

Temperate North American forest, tall pines around. Late afternoon light,
slightly overcast.
2D hand-drawn cartoon illustration, thin black outlines, flat muted colors,
subtle paper texture. No characters, NO shelter in frame.
```

> ⚠️ **Sem o abrigo laranja** — ele só aparece depois que Léo monta (roteiro, linha 60).
